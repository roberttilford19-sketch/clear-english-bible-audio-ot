# Clear English Bible — Old Testament Audio

This shelf holds the Old Testament half of the **Clear Translation Bible**, read aloud: 46 books, from Genesis to Malachi, in the Catholic order of the full 73-book Bible.

The Clear Translation Bible is a clear-English modernization of the public-domain World English Bible Classic, checked against that base verse by verse. It is not a fresh translation from the Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek, and it does not claim to be. The readings are DRAFT, awaiting the editor's read-through, and are offered freely for listening.

**Editor:** Robert Tilford.
**Reader:** Isabella — the voice of Awen, the editor's AI assistant. AI assistance in the preparation of this work is disclosed plainly, here and everywhere the work appears.

The deuterocanonical books — Tobit, Judith, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach, Baruch, and 1 and 2 Maccabees — ride with the Old Testament on this shelf, in Catholic order, as they do in the full Bible.

Eight of these books are long, so each of them stands here in two parts — Part 1 and Part 2 — so that every single file fits on the shelf; nothing is cut out, the parts simply follow one after the other. The eight are Genesis, Exodus, Numbers, 2 Chronicles, Psalms, Isaiah, Jeremiah, and Ezekiel.

The text of the whole Bible stands on its own shelf: **clear-translation-bible**. The New Testament readings stand on the companion audio shelf: **clear-translation-bible-audio-nt**. This shelf is **clear-translation-bible-audio-ot**.

Every file on this shelf is listed, with its size and length, in `MANIFEST.csv`. In all, this shelf carries about 83 hours of listening.

The shelves by their settled names: the text of the whole Bible stands at [Clear English Bible](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible); the New Testament readings stand at [Clear English Bible — New Testament Audio](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible-audio-nt); this shelf is [Clear English Bible — Old Testament Audio](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible-audio-ot).
